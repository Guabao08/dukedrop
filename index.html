Commonly asked questions

What happens if my package is lost?

You get a full refund on the price of the package itself.

What happens if my package is damaged?

You get a full refund on the service.

What happens if my package isn't delivered on time?

You get a full refund on the service.

How does DukeDrop Express work?

You ship your package to DukeDrop's address, using your own name as the recipient so it can be matched to your order. Once it arrives, DukeDrop delivers it straight to your dorm and room, no waiting through Duke's 48-hour mailroom processing.

How is Express different from Pickup?

Express skips Duke's mailroom entirely (you ship to DukeDrop instead). Pickup is for packages that are already sitting in your Duke mailroom box or locker — DukeDrop retrieves them and brings them to you.

How does Pickup work, and why do you need my Box Number or locker code?

For mailroom-box pickups, Duke requires written authorization before a third party can retrieve your package, so you'll need to text a short consent line (authorizing DukeDrop to retrieve it) along with your Box Number and which mailroom building it's in.

Do I need to provide a tracking number?

Yes, an Amazon tracking number is required for all three services (Express, Pickup, and Returns) so your order can be matched and tracked correctly.

How does the Returns service work?

Send DukeDrop your Amazon return package along with your dorm and room number, and they'll handle returning it for you. Make sure the return label is on it.

How much does DukeDrop cost?

Pricing is per package and drops the more you send in one order. Express is $5.99/package for 1–2 packages, $4.99 for 3–4, and $3.99 for 5+. Pickup is $3.99/package for 1–2, $2.99 for 3–4, and $1.99 for 5+. Return is $4.99/package for 1–2, $3.99 for 3–4, and $2.99 for 5+.

How do I pay and get in touch?

You can pay by Venmo, Zelle, or card directly through the DukeDrop Order Desk when you place your order. For questions or the mailroom pickup consent message, you can also DM @dukedrop_ on Instagram.

DukeDrop is an independent, student-run service — not affiliated with or endorsed by Duke University. Payment is not completed here; review and submit it in your payment app. Orders are not tracked in this app.
