Privacy Policy

How DukeDrop handles information when you use our site and services.

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DukeDrop (“DukeDrop,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with the DukeDrop website and delivery services. DukeDrop is an independent, student-run service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Duke University.

Information you provide

When you use the order desk, you may enter information needed to arrange a delivery, including your phone number, dorm and room, service and item counts, carrier and tracking or order numbers, and (for relevant pickups) your name, mailroom or locker location, box number, locker code, and promo code. The information requested depends on the service you select. Please provide only information needed for your order, and do not send us passwords or unrelated sensitive information.

What happens to order form information

The order form uses your entries in your browser to calculate the displayed price and prepare payment instructions. DukeDrop’s order storage feature is currently disabled. In this configuration, submitting or using the order form does not save those entries to DukeDrop’s order database. We do not claim that an order has been received or payment confirmed through the website.

The site may be configured in the future to save orders so DukeDrop can fulfill and manage them. If order storage is enabled, the information submitted with an order may be stored in a restricted-access order database and viewed by authorized DukeDrop staff for fulfillment, customer support, payment-status administration, and related operational purposes. We will update this Policy when our information practices change.

Payment apps and messages

If you choose to pay through Venmo or Zelle, the order desk prepares a payment note using some order details, such as service, quantity, dorm and room, and where relevant tracking information or pickup details. You choose whether to open the payment app and send the payment. Venmo, Zelle, and your bank process information under their own privacy policies and terms; DukeDrop does not control their handling of it.

For mailroom pickup consent, the site can open a prefilled text message when you select the text link. Your device and mobile carrier handle the message, and it is sent only when you choose to send it. You may also contact DukeDrop through Instagram; Instagram handles information you share there under its own policies.

Website operations and third parties

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts and links to third-party services, including payment apps and Instagram. Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address or browser details when your device requests their content or you follow a link. The site is configured for deployment on Vercel, which may process routine connection and security logs to operate the website. Please review the relevant providers’ privacy information for details about their practices.

We do not see analytics or advertising tracking code in the current site implementation. This statement describes the site code and may not cover technical processing by hosting, security, or linked service providers.

How we use information

We use information you provide to prepare a requested order, deliver or return packages, obtain required pickup authorization, communicate with you about service issues, and address questions or refund requests. If order storage is enabled, we may also use stored order information to administer fulfillment and payment status.

Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant details with service providers that help operate the website or complete an action you request, such as hosting, payment, messaging, or social media providers, and with authorized DukeDrop staff when needed for fulfillment or support. We may also disclose information where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect people, rights, or the service.

Retention and security

Because order storage is currently disabled, the site does not retain submitted order details in its order database in the current configuration. Details may remain in your browser, device, payment app, text messages, Instagram messages, or provider logs according to those providers’ settings and retention practices. If DukeDrop enables order storage, we will retain stored order information only as long as reasonably needed for fulfillment, support, recordkeeping, and legal obligations, and then delete or de-identify it when appropriate.

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the current service, but no website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Avoid sending locker codes or other access details through public channels.

Children’s privacy

DukeDrop’s services are intended for people age 18 or older. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to DukeDrop, contact us so we can review the situation.

Your choices and questions

You can choose not to provide optional information, though we may be unable to complete a service without details necessary to identify or deliver a package. For questions or a request about information you have shared with DukeDrop, message @dukedrop_ on Instagram. We may need to verify that a request relates to you before responding.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as our services or information practices change. We will post the current version on this page and revise the “Last updated” date. Changes apply from the date posted.

Contact

For privacy questions, message @dukedrop_ on Instagram.
